9. Use and Occupancy

The premises shall be used exclusively for lawful residential purposes by approved occupants only. Assignment, short-term rental, or subletting is prohibited unless expressly authorized in writing by Landlord.

10. Maintenance

Tenant shall keep the premises clean and promptly report maintenance issues. Landlord will perform repairs required under applicable law within a reasonable timeframe. Tenant will be liable for damages caused by negligence or misuse.

11. Entry and Notice

Landlord may enter the premises with lawful notice for inspection, repairs, maintenance, or showing the property, and may enter without prior notice in emergencies where safety or property protection is at risk.

12. Damage, Liability, and Repairs

Tenant is responsible for maintaining the premises in good order and for damage caused by Tenant, occupants, or guests beyond normal wear and tear. Tenant must promptly notify Landlord of hazards, leaks, or needed repairs to mitigate further damage.

13. Utilities Allocation Schedule

Utility responsibility shall be allocated by written schedule or listing terms, including electricity, gas, water, internet, and waste. Unpaid tenant-assigned utilities may be treated as additional rent where legally permitted.

14. Insurance

Tenant is encouraged, and may be required where lawful, to maintain renter's insurance for personal property, temporary housing, and personal liability. Landlord's insurance does not cover Tenant's personal belongings.

15. Default and Remedies

Failure to pay rent, repeated lease violations, unauthorized occupancy, or other material breach constitutes default. Landlord may pursue all remedies allowed by applicable law, including notices to cure, charges, and termination processes.

16. Early Termination

Early termination by Tenant requires written notice and may incur lawful fees, rent responsibility until re-rental, or other charges permitted by law, unless statutory exceptions apply.

17. Renewal and Holdover

Renewal terms must be documented in writing. If Tenant remains in possession after lease expiration without a signed renewal, tenancy may convert to holdover or month-to-month status where allowed, under revised rental terms and notice requirements.

18. Compliance with Laws and Community Rules

Tenant, occupants, and guests shall comply with all laws, health and safety requirements, and building/community rules. Nuisance, excessive noise, violence, harassment, and dangerous behavior are prohibited.

The use, possession, manufacture, distribution, or sale of illegal drugs or controlled substances, and any illegal activity of any kind on or near the premises, is strictly prohibited and constitutes a material lease breach subject to immediate legal remedies.

19. Move-In and Move-Out Inspection

Parties should complete move-in and move-out condition reports with dated photos where practical. These records may be used to assess cleaning, repairs, and lawful security deposit deductions.

20. Dispute Resolution and Governing Venue

The parties agree to attempt good-faith resolution of disputes before formal proceedings. Where lawful and agreed, disputes may be submitted to mediation or arbitration. Otherwise, disputes shall be resolved in the courts of competent jurisdiction where the premises are located.

21. Notices

Official notices under this Agreement must be delivered using legally recognized methods (for example, hand delivery, email where permitted, portal delivery, or mail) to addresses/contact points designated by each party.

Parties acknowledge that lease, payment, screening, maintenance, and signature records may be processed and stored electronically for contract administration, legal compliance, and audit purposes, subject to applicable privacy and data protection laws.

23. Entire Agreement and Severability

This Agreement, including incorporated schedules and addenda, constitutes the entire agreement between parties regarding the tenancy. If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, remaining provisions continue in full force to the maximum extent permitted by law.

26. Unauthorized Occupants and Subletting

27. Returned Payment, NSF, and Chargebacks

28. No Waiver

Landlord's delay, partial enforcement, or prior acceptance of noncompliant conduct does not waive any rights. All rights and remedies remain cumulative unless expressly waived in writing.
